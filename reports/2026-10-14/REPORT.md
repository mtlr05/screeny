## 1. Screening

A post must be: free to read in full (paywalled or preview-only posts are dropped); a single-stock bullish pitch (updates and earnings reviews count); on a small/mid-cap company; listed in the US or Canada; and the author must disclose holding a long position (a Seeking Alpha "beneficial long position" disclosure, or an explicit "I own / I am long / I hold a position" statement; "may hold" boilerplate does not count). Any length qualifies. Lacking a moat or an edge is not a drop.

## 2. Reported, not scored

Fact-check of key claims, author batting average (share of past long pitches that beat SPY, or XIC.TO for Canadian listings, over 1-year and 2-year windows; see the Author batting average skill), management guidance record, balance-sheet flags, and soft notes. Shown per name; they do not change the score.

## 3. Scoring (out of 6, 1 point each)

1. **Thesis with secular runway**: cheap with no growth = 0.5.
2. **Return ~40%/yr**: from the publish-day close to the author's target over the author's timeframe (2 years if none given). 25-40%/yr = 0.5; under 25%/yr = 0.
3. **CEO is a large shareholder** (roughly 5%+ = 1; 1-5% = 0.5).
4. **Moat or edge**: moat graded on the five moat types (Strong 1 / Moderate 0.75 / Narrow 0.5), or an edge: proven operator, allocator or dealmaker, or an obsessive founder-builder (a good guidance record supports it).
5. **Differential view**: names why the market is wrong (stigma or label, temporary or mundane issue, neglect) and why it corrects; an overreaction to temporary news supports it.
6. **Dated catalyst within ~24 months**: undated = 0.5.

Ranking: best article per ticker; top 15 plus all ties at 15th (cut = 4), ties alphabetical.
